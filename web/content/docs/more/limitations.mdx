---
title: "Limitations"
description: "What Suspect does not do, stated plainly."
---

- **It narrows, it does not discover.** Suspect tests the hypotheses people propose against the clues people pin. A cause nobody proposed is not in the board.
- **It does not verify a clue.** An observation is treated as true. A false clue pinned in good faith or bad eliminates honest theories until someone pins a clearer one.
- **It does not weigh likelihood.** A leading hypothesis is the one the given evidence supports best, not a proven cause, and a very broad hypothesis can survive because nothing contradicts it. A precise rival that explains the same clues ties it.
- **The label is a model reading.** Validators agree on one label per pair, so the matrix is as good as that reading. The golden run is published with its misses; two pairs the spec expected to be CONTRADICTS came out SILENT, see the [evaluation](/docs/more/evaluation).
- **Capped on purpose.** Six clues and six hypotheses make at most 36 pairs, so a board always fits a budget.
- **Public and permanent.** Clues and hypotheses cannot be edited or removed.
