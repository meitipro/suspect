## Is a leading hypothesis a verdict?

No. It is the hypothesis the pinned clues support best. Suspect narrows theories by the evidence given.

## Who can pin a clue?

Anyone, for 1 GEN, which comes back when the board resolves.

## What if nobody resolves?

The owner has 7 days. After that anyone may resolve in favour of the leading hypothesis, and when none is leading, anyone may resolve with no cause and every bond returns.

## Can I challenge an elimination?

Yes, by pinning a clearer observation. Every pair, label and observation is public, so the clue that eliminated a theory can be argued with.

## Which network is it on?

GenLayer Studio Next, chain 61997, on the GenVM v0.4 runtime.

## Why can only some pairs be checked?

Any pair can be checked, once. Only the label of a pair is stored; nothing the validators wrote as a reason is kept on chain.
