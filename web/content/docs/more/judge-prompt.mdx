## What the judge never sees

The prompt carries one hypothesis and one observation and nothing else: not the other clues, not the other suspects, not the bonds, not who wrote what. It judges consistency only, never whether a hypothesis is likely.

## What validators agree on

The leader and every validator ask the same prompt and answer one label. A validator agrees only when its own label is the leader's: `mine["label"] == theirs.get("label")`, with no tolerance. Elimination and ranking are then code: any CONTRADICTS eliminates a hypothesis, and the survivors are ranked by how many clues they explain.
