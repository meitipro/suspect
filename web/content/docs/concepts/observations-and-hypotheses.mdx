---
title: "Observations and hypotheses"
description: "What goes on a board, and the limits the contract enforces."
---

A board holds two kinds of text. Both are public, one line each, at most 200 characters.

| | Observation (a clue) | Hypothesis (a suspect) |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A fact recorded about the incident | A proposed cause |
| Who adds it | Anyone | Anyone, until the owner locks the board |
| Cost | 1 GEN, returned when the board resolves | A bond of at least 3 GEN |
| Limit | 6 per board | 6 per board, 2 per author |
| Editable | No, and it cannot be removed | No |

Observations are what every hypothesis is tested against, so they must be hard to rig. Any member can pin one, which means the owner cannot keep a clue out by not pinning it, and a clue is public with its author. If a clue is unclear, pin a clearer one: the pair is judged again against it.

The title of a board is at most 80 characters. The board opens a 7 day window when it is created, and `lock_board` closes it to new hypotheses (the owner only); clues can still be pinned and pairs can still be checked on a locked board.

Hypotheses are about causes, not people. Boards are public and permanent, so pin only what can be public.
